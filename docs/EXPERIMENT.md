# Commercial experiment

## Hypothesis

Etsy sellers who price handmade or digital listings will pay $19 once for KeepFloor Catalog because Offsite Ads can erase profit, and we can reach them through a useful free calculator on a purchase-intent page plus (if authorized) a marketplace listing.

## Assumptions most likely to kill it

1. Free calculators are enough; nobody pays $19.  
2. Not enough qualified sellers see the tool (channel failure, not product failure).  
3. A fee-table error or Etsy change destroys trust and causes refunds.

## Smallest credible test

**Measure:** completed calculations (organic + safe floor shown), catalog CTA clicks, checkout starts, paid orders.  
**Audience:** people who arrive with an Etsy pricing/Offsite Ads problem — not generic homepage tourists.  
**Money:** $0 unless you authorize hosting (free tier) or an Etsy listing ($0.20 + possible shop set-up fee shown at onboarding).  
**Time:** ~2 hours to connect Polar sandbox, paste checkout URL, deploy static files if you authorize deploy.

| Outcome | Threshold | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Success | 1 genuine paid order **or** 3 checkout starts in 30 days after the page has 80+ completed calculations | Keep, then improve listing/SEO |
| Failure (no demand) | ≥200 completed calculations from likely sellers and **0** checkout starts | Kill or replace the offer |
| Failure (no exposure) | <50 completed calculations in 30 days live | Do not call it a demand failure; change channel (marketplace listing, different query) |
| Inconclusive | 50–199 completions and 1–2 CTA clicks, no checkout | Extend 30 days or add the backup listing; do not spend on ads yet |

A product that nobody sees has not failed a demand test.

Prepared assets: working calculator, catalog, sample CSV, listing drafts in `docs/listings/`. **Not sent or published.**
